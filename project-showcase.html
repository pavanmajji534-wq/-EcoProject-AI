3. The 6-Point Systematic Architecture Matrix

Every generated project answers the critical engineering questions required by academic juries and grant evaluators:

1. What Is Monitored?

Pinpoints the exact physical medium (e.g., plate-waste weight, ultrasonic flow pulse, PM2.5 particle density).

2. Who Uses The System?

Identifies exact on-ground stakeholders (dining managers, plumbers, security gate marshals, municipal crews).

3. What Data Is Collected?

Specifies telemetry units: differential pressure ($\Delta P$), timestamps, GPS coordinates, scrap grams.

4. How Technology Is Applied?

Articulates algorithms: time-series forecasting, TinyML vision segmentation, rule-based threshold relays.

5. What Action Is Taken?

Closed-loop mitigation: motorized solenoid shutoff, prep reduction alerts, surplus food shelter dispatch.

6. Environmental Reduction

Quantified metrics: $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$ offsets, liters conserved, landfill diversion percentage.
