1. Problem Statement & Context

The systemic limitation in current sustainability ideation tools and on-ground environmental bottlenecks.

❌ The Problem: Template Fatigue & Disconnect

  • Generic Platitudes: Conventional AI tools output superficial advice like "Use AI to reduce waste" without actionable technical specifications.
  • Ignoring Root Causes: They address symptoms (e.g. food in trash) rather than root causes (e.g. attendance volatility, lack of cold-storage).
  • Context Blindness: They propose high-power cloud setups in rural farming villages experiencing 6-hour daily blackouts.
  • Hallucinatory Fallbacks: They force non-environmental queries (crypto, gaming) into arbitrary, fabricated eco-solutions.

✅ EcoProject AI Intervention

  • Dynamic Diagnostic Reasoning: Zero predefined boilerplate; parses specific operational failure modes.
  • Logically Connected Chain: Preserves strict causal coherence: Problem → Causes → Impacts → Solution.
  • Strict Environmental Gate: Rejects non-sustainability prompts with guided feedback.
  • Constraint-Aware Sizing: Tailors Bill of Materials (BOM) to target setting and difficulty.

2. Objectives

Measurable technical goals established to deliver rigorous, jury-ready sustainability proposals.

🛡️ 1. Relevance Gate

Automatically classify inputs as ENVIRONMENTAL or NON_ENVIRONMENTAL; block non-sustainability inputs to maintain scientific integrity.

🔬 2. Root-Cause Isolation

Differentiate between visible symptoms and physical/behavioral failure modes (e.g. concealed riser ruptures, student turnout variance, gate idling queues).

🔗 3. Causal Solution Mapping

Guarantee that every single engineering feature directly neutralizes a specific diagnosed cause with zero disconnected filler.

📐 4. 6-Point Matrix Sizing

Answer: What is monitored, Who uses it, What data is collected, How tech is applied, What action is taken, and How impact is reduced.

⚙️ 5. Multi-Tier Calibration

Calibrate hardware (Arduino vs ESP32 vs Jetson) and software to three scopes: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

📥 6. Academic Export

Deliver download-ready Markdown proposals, structured JSON, and PDF-ready technical reports for capstone submissions.

3. Methodology

A 4-stage natural language and engineering pipeline executed on every user submission.

1️⃣
Classification

Parses input tokens against environmental taxonomies. Disqualifies non-environmental queries with guided feedback.

2️⃣
Decomposition

Extracts specific resource streams (greywater, food scraps, PM2.5) and local constraints (power cuts, no Wi-Fi, low budget).

3️⃣
Causal Synthesis

Synthesizes formal problem statement, root causes, operational impacts, and paired technical countermeasures.

4️⃣
Stack Sizing

Sizes hardware, software, protocols, and computes quantified environmental offsets and Bill of Materials (BOM).

Core Novelty • Slide 5 of 7

5. Innovation

Key architectural differentiators that separate EcoProject AI from traditional chatbots.

🧠 Dual-Engine Architecture

Combines a Zero-Latency Built-in Semantic Analyst (running 100% offline in browser with zero API dependencies) with optional Live Google Gemini 3.6 Flash reasoning, complete with automatic fallback.

🎯 1:1 Cause-and-Effect Matrix

Replaces vague summaries with explicit paired countermeasure blocks. If Cause A is "lack of localized cold storage", Countermeasure A is "an automated surplus dispatch alert notifying local shelters within a 3-hour window."

📊 Adaptive Bill of Materials (BOM)

Answers the #1 jury question: "How much will this cost?" Synthesizes realistic hardware components, software licenses, and estimated price ranges calibrated to student budgets vs commercial scale.

🚫 Anti-Hallucination Rejection Gate

Unlike ChatGPT or generic LLMs that attempt to answer any prompt, EcoProject AI strictly enforces domain relevance, preventing non-environmental contamination.
